❌ What users write negatively
(Based on 26 negative reviews)

- Account Freezing and Closure**
- Users frequently report that their accounts are frozen without explanation, leading to significant frustration and financial disruption. Several reviews mention unexpected account closures and prolonged holds on funds.
- Customer Service Issues**
- Many users express dissatisfaction with customer support, citing unhelpful responses, lack of accountability, and difficulty in reaching a live representative. Complaints highlight poor communication regarding account issues and inadequate support for resolving problems.
- High Fees and Unexpected Charges**
- Numerous reviewers complain about sudden fee increases for account services, particularly for users who require minimal transactions. There are mentions of additional costs for services like LEI numbers and frustrations over being charged for suspended services.
- Technical Problems with the App**
- Users report glitches and a non-intuitive interface, making navigation difficult. Specific issues include problems logging in, app crashes during account setup, and compatibility issues with devices.
- Documentation and Verification Difficulties**
- Several users mention problems with the document verification process for business accounts, with claims that their submitted documentation is repeatedly deemed insufficient despite meeting the requirements.
- Lack of Transparency and Trust**
- There are concerns about the reliability of Revolut as a banking provider, with users feeling unsure about the safety of their funds. Complaints emphasize a lack of transparency regarding account management and the reasons for account actions.
- Decreased Perks and Increased Restrictions**
- Users have noted a decline in benefits associated with their accounts, with more restrictions and higher fees introduced over time. This has contributed to a sense of dissatisfaction among long-term customers.
