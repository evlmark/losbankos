❌ What users write negatively
(Based on 23 negative reviews)

- Poor Customer Support**
- Users report a lack of available help, with many unable to contact a representative or receive timely responses. Complaints include rude service representatives and unhelpful automated responses.
- Account Closure Issues**
- Several users experienced sudden account closures without explanation, often feeling that their accounts were terminated arbitrarily. This has led to frustration over the inability to retrieve funds or understand the reasons behind the closures.
- High and Hidden Fees**
- Users express dissatisfaction with unexpected fees, including commissions for transactions and charges for suspended services. There are complaints about the increasing costs associated with maintaining an account.
- Technical Difficulties and User Interface Problems**
- Many reviewers highlight a non-intuitive interface and glitches within the app, including issues with account activation and error messages preventing access. Complaints about technical difficulties have also been frequent.
- Complicated Account Setup and Verification**
- Users faced challenges during account setup, particularly with providing documentation to prove business legitimacy. This process is described as cumbersome, leading to account activation delays.
- Limited Functionality for US Users**
- Specific complaints mention the lack of IBAN support for US-based users, resulting in slow and expensive transaction methods for European clients.
- Decreasing Benefits and Increasing Restrictions**
- Users have noted a decline in perks and an increase in restrictions over time, which has led to dissatisfaction with the overall service provided by Revolut.
- Unclear Communication and Policies**
- Many users reported a lack of clear communication from the company regarding policies, fees, and account status, leading to confusion and mistrust.
